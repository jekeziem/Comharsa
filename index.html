comharsa is irish for neighbour. it's a room for your street that opens when the power goes. no signal, no wi-fi, no account. phones pass messages to each other over bluetooth.

the room finds you.

when the power and signal drop, phones on the same street find each other over bluetooth and pass messages along, house to house. nobody downloads anything mid-storm.

milling · mileti

built for checking with each other.

when people get a warning, they don't act straight away. they check with others first. so every update shows how many neighbours have seen it, and it only counts as confirmed once three have.

the bystander effect · latané & darley

"someone else will check on her."

the more people who could help, the less each one feels it's on them. so check-ins say out loud who hasn't been heard from, and it stops being someone else's job to go and knock.

safety first

a map that forgets.

a map of who's home in a storm is also a map of who's alone. so only the room sees it, anyone can hide their house, and it's gone 24 hours after the lights come back.

why i made this

when something goes wrong, people don't need a smarter system. they need to know the people next door are ok. comharsa just makes the knock easier.
